The Founder

From the Skies
to the Algorithm

Zay is a flight attendant from Queens, New York — and like most people in this city, he was doing everything himself. Managing his own business, handling the admin, the outreach, the content. All of it. On top of a full-time schedule that kept him 30,000 feet in the air half the week.

So he sat down and put AI to work on his own business — the website, the content, the research, the day-to-day. Not because he had a computer science degree or unlimited resources, but because he refused to believe that kind of leverage was only for big corporations with big budgets.

He was right. And once it was working for him, the question became obvious: Why aren't small businesses getting this?

That question became ZayOS. A company built with one mission — to grow every small business in New York (and beyond) with the same edge the big players have had for years: a website that converts, content that compounds, and decisions backed by data. Custom built. Always on. NYC native.
