The Founder

From the Skies
to the Algorithm

Zay is a flight attendant from Queens, New York — and like most people in this city, he was doing everything himself. Managing his own business, handling the admin, the outreach, the content. All of it. On top of a full-time schedule that kept him 30,000 feet in the air half the week.

He sat down and built an AI team from scratch. Not because he had a computer science degree or unlimited resources — but because he refused to believe that advanced AI operations were only for big corporations with big budgets.

He was right. And once it was working for him, the question became obvious: Why aren't small businesses getting this?

That question became ZayOS. A company built with one mission — to give every small business in New York (and beyond) the same AI-powered edge that Fortune 500 companies have had for years. Custom built. Always on. NYC native.
